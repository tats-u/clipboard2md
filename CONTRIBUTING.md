The body of a PR must be manually assembled by a human — meaning that while AI-generated text may be pasted or used as a base, the act of pasting into the input field and the final review must be performed by hand as long as you understand all the assembled contents — and the PR must be submitted by a deliberate human action such as pressing Enter or an equivalent operation, whether via the web interface, CLI, or API. The author (human) of a PR is accountable for every changed line.

PRs created in a fully automated manner without prior approval from a maintainer will be closed immediately upon discovery.

In particular, PRs created based on https://github.com/charles-openclaw/charles-microbounties, or on any similar reward or incentive program under which it is impossible or effectively impossible for the maintainer to receive a share of the compensation — whether due to the program's structure or to the maintainer's own circumstances — will result in the author's GitHub account being blocked without warning or appeal. Such cases may also be reported to GitHub.

Rule revisions become effective for existing issues or PRs from the day after the corresponding commit is merged into the default branch, and are immediately effective for newly created issues and pull requests. For the purposes of this rule, "the day after" means the same clock time on the following calendar day in UTC, adjusted for any leap units (e.g. leap seconds), and is not guaranteed to equal exactly 86,400 seconds. PRs created or remaining open after this grace period will be subject to the above sanctions.
